System Utilities

Google Drive Authorisation

Required once before DocVault can extract .gdoc, .gsheet, .gslides, and other Google Drive stub files. A browser window will open so you can sign in and grant read-only access to your Drive. The token is saved locally and reused automatically — you only need to do this once.

Qdrant Health Check

This tool will attempt to connect to your Qdrant database, write a temporary data point, read it back, and then delete it. This verifies connectivity and basic operations.

Diagnostics

Clear the logs database (task timings, worker errors, system stats). This does not affect your documents or settings — only observability data.
